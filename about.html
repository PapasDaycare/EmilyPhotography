Behind the camera

Hi, I’m Emily.

Hi, I’m Emily Hopp, a professional photographer based in Posen, Michigan, serving all of Northern Michigan. Photography isn’t just my job—it’s my passion and my way of preserving the moments that matter most. I’m married to my wonderful husband, and together we’re raising our two amazing kids—a daughter and a son. Being a mom has given me a deep appreciation for how quickly time moves, and it’s what fuels my love for capturing genuine connection, emotion, and real-life memories. I grew up in Alpena and have spent my entire life exploring Northern Michigan. Having local knowledge of the area and its hidden gems allows me to choose beautiful locations that truly fit each client’s vision—whether it’s a cozy family session, a couple’s shoot, or a timeless portrait. My style focuses on natural expressions, soft lighting, and images that feel authentic rather than overly posed. I strive to create a relaxed and enjoyable experience so you feel comfortable in front of the camera—and confident in the final images you receive. I would be honored to tell your story through photographs you’ll treasure for years to come.

What you can expect

• A quick planning chat before your session
• Gentle direction and posing help throughout
• Clean edits with true-to-life color and flattering tones
• A gallery you’ll actually want to share
